Supplier Stack-Up Example data

Supplier partnership segmentation across four perspectives. Nothing is sent anywhere.

This tool takes a group through choosing the 1–3 suppliers to build a partnership with. It runs in four steps, one subcategory at a time, and ends with a summary of what was agreed.

1

Setup & weighting

Upload the supplier list. The tool lists the subcategories in it. Tick Include in workshop for the subcategories you want to cover. Then click a subcategory, tick Include in scoring for the suppliers to score, and set how much each of the four dimensions matters.

Outcome: agreed scope (subcategories and suppliers) and weights per subcategory.

2

Supplier criteria scoring

Score every included supplier in the selected subcategory from 1 to 5, one criterion per screen, with a comment on why. When the last criterion is done, the subcategory is ticked off and you return to step 1 for the next one.

Outcome: a ranking and segment per subcategory.

3

Supplier evaluations

Appears when every included subcategory is evaluated. Look at the overall picture across the included subcategories, check close calls, and adjust scores or weights where the room disagrees.

Outcome: a result the room stands behind.

4

Final selection

Compare the finalists from the included subcategories and decide: a) which suppliers to work with, b) who to involve internally, c) the next steps. One button then gives the workshop summary.

Outcome: decisions, owners and a summary to share.

Good to know

  • In the room: a facilitator, and the people who know the suppliers (category, quality, R&D, supply chain).
  • Scores start empty for every supplier and are set by the room in step 2. An empty score counts as 0 until it is set.
  • Your data stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use "Clear my data" in the top bar when you are done.
  • Example data is loaded so you can try the flow first. Uploading your own supplier list replaces it.

Weights

Why we weight. The four dimensions do not matter equally in every subcategory. In one, security of supply is the main concern; in another, innovation or cost. The weights are how the room agrees up front what a good partner looks like here, before looking at any supplier. That keeps the scoring honest: suppliers are judged against what we said matters, not the other way round.

Set each dimension from 0 (does not matter here) to 10 (critical). Agree the weights first, then score.

Import data

Paste CSV (or pick a file). First row: Supplier then one column per criterion. Optional rows: Perspective, Weight (0-10), Knock-out (minimum score), Definition, Score 1 means … Score 5 means, Stakeholders (e.g. Head of Quality [Validate]; Site planner [Provide input, done]). Optional columns: Comment: <criterion> and Overall note. Every other row is a supplier with scores 1-5. Export first to get a template with the current criteria.
